# 

**Serves:** 2
**Time:** ~30 minutes

## Ingredients

### Pork

* 300 g pork shoulder/bondiola/paleta
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp sugar
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* 1 garlic clove, finely grated or minced
* 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
* 1 tsp neutral oil

### Broth

* 750 ml good chicken or pork stock
* 1–1½ tbsp white or yellow miso
* 1½ tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp sugar
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* 1 tsp rice vinegar
* 1 small piece kombu, optional
* 1 garlic clove, lightly crushed
* 2–3 thin slices fresh ginger
* ½ tsp fish sauce, optional
* ½ tsp MSG, optional

### Toppings

* 2 eggs
* 100–150 g mushrooms, preferably shiitake
* 2–3 spring onions
* 1 sheet nori, cut into pieces
* Chili oil or chili crisp, to taste
* Sesame seeds, optional

### Noodles

* 2 portions ramen noodles, fresh or dried

---

## 1. Make the eggs

Bring a saucepan of water to a vigorous boil.

Lower in the eggs and cook for **6½ minutes**.

Transfer immediately to cold water. Peel once cool.

For a quick marinade, mix:

* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp water
* ½ tsp sugar

Put the peeled eggs in the mixture while you prepare the rest.

---

## 2. Start the broth

Put the stock in a saucepan with:

* kombu, if using
* 1 crushed garlic clove
* 2–3 slices ginger

Bring almost to a simmer and cook gently for **10 minutes**.

Remove the kombu.

Mix in:

* miso
* soy sauce
* sugar
* fish sauce, if using
* MSG, if using

Keep the broth hot but **don't boil it hard after adding the miso**.

At the end, add the sesame oil and rice vinegar.

Taste and adjust. It should be strongly savory because the noodles will dilute the flavor slightly.

---

## 3. Cook the pork

Slice the pork into pieces about **1 cm thick**.

Heat a frying pan over medium-high/high heat. Add the neutral oil.

Sear the pork until **deeply browned** on both sides, about 2–3 minutes per side.

Add:

* garlic
* ginger
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp sugar
* 2 tbsp water

Reduce the heat and toss the pork in the sauce for **2–3 minutes**, until glossy and caramelized.

Remove the pork and keep warm.

**Don't clean the pan.**

---

## 4. Brown the mushrooms

Slice the mushrooms.

Put them directly into the pork pan and cook over medium-high heat for **4–5 minutes**, letting them brown well.

The leftover pork glaze will coat them.

---

## 5. Make the aromatic oil

Finely slice one spring onion.

Put it in a small heatproof bowl with:

* a little minced garlic
* chili flakes, if desired

Heat about **1 tbsp neutral oil** until quite hot.

Pour the oil over the spring onion and garlic.

It should sizzle.

This takes about 30 seconds and adds a surprisingly large amount of flavor.

---

## 6. Cook the noodles

Cook the ramen noodles **separately** according to their instructions.

Drain well.

Don't cook them in the broth—the starch will make the broth cloudy and can make the noodles overcooked.

---

## 7. Assemble

Divide the noodles between two large bowls.

Pour over the hot broth.

Arrange on top:

* glazed pork
* browned mushrooms
* halved eggs
* sliced spring onions
* pieces of nori

Add:

* ½–1 tsp aromatic oil per bowl
* chili oil/chili crisp to taste
* sesame seeds, if using

Serve immediately.

## The important details

**Brown the pork hard.** This gives you much more flavor than simply simmering it.

**Don't boil the miso.** Add it toward the end.

**Brown the mushrooms separately.** Their flavor is much better this way.

**Season the broth slightly aggressively.** The noodles and toppings will dilute it.

**The rice vinegar matters.** Just a teaspoon cuts through the richness and makes the whole bowl taste brighter.

### Optional upgrade

If you have **tahini or Chinese sesame paste**, add **1 teaspoon per bowl** to the broth. It makes the ramen richer and creamier without requiring a long-cooked pork broth.
